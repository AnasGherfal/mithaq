# Deferred and rejected features

The following features are intentionally absent from Milestone 1 and must not be
inferred from the foundation UI.

## Stage A, later milestones

- public trust and policy pages;
- 18+ gate;
- phone OTP and returning sessions;
- serious-intention waitlist questionnaire;
- consent history;
- referral attribution;
- waitlist status and deletion request;
- invitation-only aggregate administration.

## Stage B, only after validation

- member profiles and preferences;
- government-ID and liveness verification;
- private photographs and signed access;
- curated introductions and compatibility rules;
- mutual-interest decisions;
- messaging and structured prompts;
- family-member invitations and group conversations;
- blocking, reporting, moderation, suspension, bans, and appeals;
- notifications;
- data export;
- subscriptions and payments.

## Rejected product patterns

- infinite swiping;
- a public member directory;
- public profile photographs;
- open unsolicited direct messaging;
- public likes, comments, followers, rankings, or popularity counts;
- streaks, coins, gifts, flames, or addictive engagement mechanics;
- AI-generated deceptive romantic messages;
- automated claims that an algorithm predicts a successful marriage;
- safety, privacy, blocking, or reporting behind a paywall;
- family control over an adult member without explicit consent;
- screenshot-proof or absolute-security promises.
